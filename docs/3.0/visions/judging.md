# How the 3.0 direction was chosen

Five independent "architect" agents each wrote a complete 3.0 vision from a deliberately different angle. All of them had the same research and the same constraints. Three judge agents then scored every vision, each through a different lens. A synthesizer took the best-scoring base, grafted on the strongest ideas from the others, and wrote the [North Star](../north-star.md).

## Judge: hiring

*Lens:* Would a staff engineer / hiring manager at a top company be impressed, and would every claim survive an interview? (depth, uniqueness, credibility, demo-ability, measurable claims, breadth, storytelling)

**Verdict.** Winner: systems ('Seismograph'), 58/70, narrowly ahead of contrarian (56). Experience and ai-native tie at 53; intelligence scores 49. Seismograph is the only vision whose numbers were actually measured. I confirmed that research/scripts/measure_gkg.py and backfill_size.py exist and compute exactly the metrics the document tags [measured], and the 58.5%/50.9% city-level figure matches the independent GDELT research. It is also the only vision whose build/no-build calls already rest on benchmarks it ran. The Rust parser is rejected at 12-14k rows/s/core against a 5.67 TB backfill, and a Rust clustering kernel waits behind a replay-time trigger with >=99.9% differential agreement. It alone has a provable correctness claim (exactly-once effects under 1,000 SIGKILLs, with identical state hashes) and turns propagation into a calibrated measurement (a crawl-lag model with interval coverage). It spans systems, data and ML roles, and every bullet survives a 30-minute grilling. Its two weaknesses are the thinnest UX of the five and bloated scope, and both can be fixed by grafting and cutting.

Final direction: Seismograph's engine is the spine: the ledger and chaos harness, StoryStream feeding Magnitude and Wavefront, and Rust only behind its trigger. Experience provides the face: the first five seconds, develop-on-settle, precision-sized point blur for location honesty, CDN-static time-cubes, the Shot contract and the virtual-clock renderer. Contrarian supplies the signature finding: exposure in stops replaces every vision's vague Infrared, with sensor-only severity, quantification intervals and a reproducible weekly report attested to Sigstore. AI-native contributes its eval harness for a late, bounded agent and tour layer that is never the headline. Intelligence contributes bitemporal as-of and 'computed first, generated second'.

Cut hard: UCDP/VIEWS forecasting, accounts and inboxes, the entity graph, Boards, Figures, and vanity graphics. Cap ML at two depth pillars, StoryStream > Wavefront and Exposure. Suggested pitch: 'A seismograph for the world's news: where every story starts, how it travels, and where the world isn't looking, measured, with error bars.'

**Must avoid:**

- Leading with 'an AI that drives the map'. World Monitor's Analyst and its 33 WebMCP tools already own it, and kepler.gl shipped a tour editor on 28 Oct 2026. The agent is a late, evaluated layer, never the headline or the 10-second demo.
- Any event forecasting: UCDP country-month predictions vs VIEWS, or 'early warning' framed as prediction. GDELT measures attention, not ground truth; it is padding and a likely public loss.
- Shipping every research-grade ML feature (StoryStream, Perspectives/Prism, Gap/Infrared, Signals, Figures, an entity graph). Pick two depth pillars, StoryStream > Wavefront and Exposure; everything else is explicitly cuttable and ordered.
- Accounts, a separate auth service, inboxes, Web Push, Boards and team features before launch. Use URLs, Atom feeds and webhooks, and keep the GDPR surface at zero.
- Any technology without a fired, measured trigger and a before/after ADR (Rust, C++, Kubernetes, Kafka/NATS, ClickHouse, Qdrant, WebGPU in the core). If the trigger never fires, it never goes on the resume.
- Presenting targets, illustrative readouts ('-4.6 stops', '+2.7 sigma') or numbers measured by research agents as personal measurements. Every README and resume number comes from a script in bench/ or the metrics ledger.
- Gold sets that cannot be honestly labeled: 1,000-1,500 items in 12 languages, or a kappa score with no real second annotator. Size gold sets at roughly 300-500 items, machine-translation-assisted, with a recruited second annotator, and document how they were made.
- Raw-volume encodings (they map where newsrooms are), centroid pins, and any interpolation across GDELT's June 2025 gap.
- Storing full text, quoting sources in AI text, per-article summaries, publisher thumbnails by default, or severity derived from news coverage (circularity).
- Three overlapping orchestration layers (a custom ledger, pgmq and Dagster). Use the ledger plus pgmq for the stream; adopt Dagster only past ~5 interdependent scheduled assets, with an ADR.
- Vendor and naming sprawl: four compute providers (Vercel, Hetzner, Cloud Run, Modal) or a dozen coined terms (bezel, detent, bokeh, Director, Globe Cast, Satellite Day, Tripod, Constellation). Keep one metaphor family and at most 4-5 modes.
- Budgeting LLM spend up to the $50 ceiling or leaving judge and eval costs out of the budget. Hard-cap steady-state LLM spend at about $20/mo, count evals, and serve cached tours on landing.
- Graphics vanity that costs weeks and adds no insight (4K accumulation stills, nightly software-GL MP4s, Satellite Day, 'attention weather' particles) before the core Lens, Wavefront and Exposure are shipped and measured.
- Leaderboards of suffering, or a value-laden severity choice presented as objective. Compare languages by default, publish switchable severity definitions with intervals, and write 'coverage', never 'worth'.
- Self-graded agent benchmarks without frozen snapshots, a published method and model-comparison baselines.
- Depending on Hetzner CAX availability without a tested fallback in IaC.

## Judge: feasibility

*Lens:* Can one developer with Claude Code build and run it? (solo-buildable, cost, ops burden, tech risk, shippability, legal exposure, scope creep)

**Verdict.** Winner: contrarian ('Underexposed') with 55/70. The others: experience 42, ai-native 35, systems 32, intelligence 30. Each total is the sum of seven 1–10 scores, where 10 is best (most buildable, cheapest, least ops, lowest risk, most shippable, lowest legal exposure, least scope creep).

Underexposed is the only vision that is complete and unique by phase 4. It targets the one verified white space nobody publishes in real time, attention versus severity. It is also the smallest system: a browser-side Lens over CDN cubes, no vector DB, crawler, broker or accounts, a Postgres that can be rebuilt from the lake, about $20–37/month, and the cleanest copyright posture.

The others fail a solo developer in predictable ways:
- Intelligence is a SaaS with on-call delivery SLOs.
- Systems spends its timeline on rigor users never see, although its measured numbers reproduced exactly when I re-ran its script.
- AI-native's hero is the least differentiated feature and the only one whose cost scales per visitor.
- Experience has the best front-end architecture but stakes its identity on experimental GPU-globe craft.

Underexposed is not safe as written. Its existential risk is the severity denominator: one commensurable 'people affected' measure, with protracted crises depending on the unverified HDX HAPI. That needs a week-1 spike plus a published sensor-only variant. It also drops the story-level 'world news on a globe' feel the user loves.

Final direction: keep Underexposed's thesis, scope rule and data plane, and graft onto it:
- Systems: the ledger with exactly-once effects, the crawl-lag model and llm_memo.
- Experience: the Shot contract, the immutable-bucket CDN path, the no-DB Phase 1, location-honesty optics and the develop-on-settle landing.
- Intelligence: set-based watch-lenses and the Sigstore-attested weekly report.
- AI-native: Globe Script and grader patterns, reserved for an optional late-phase Analyst behind a spend governor.
- Story depth without StoryStream's cost: lag-corrected per-crisis propagation arcs, plus a SemEval-2022 T8 embedding bake-off for a public benchmark line.

**Must avoid:**

- User accounts, push notifications, email digests, Morning Briefs with delivery SLOs, and Boards. These are user-facing on-call promises a solo developer cannot keep; watch-lenses stay URLs with Atom feeds and webhooks.
- Making a per-visitor LLM chat agent the hero feature. World Monitor already ships a map-moving analyst, and per-visitor cost would force rationing of the headline feature on launch day. Agents come late, behind cached tours and a spend governor.
- Any lag, propagation, first-seen or attention-cliff metric without crawl-lag correction and intervals. Measured median GDELT crawl lag is 82–120 min with p90 ~5 h, so Intelligence's ±15 min assumption is about 10x too optimistic.
- Article text in any form: no transient body fetches or Tier-1 crawlers, no displayed RSS ledes, no publisher thumbnails by default. Keep titles, URLs and derived numbers only, and make any AI text multi-source, labelled 'AI-generated from N sources', quote-free and checked for 8-gram overlap.
- Infrastructure whose trigger has not fired: Dagster, DuckLake, Kafka or NATS, SSE fan-out, ClickHouse, Qdrant or any separate vector DB, Kubernetes, Rust/C++/WASM, WebGPU in the core. A ledger, cron and Parquet on R2 cover the measured load.
- Running several ML research programs at once (Systems' 11 components, Intelligence's 13 features, framing diffs plus forecasts plus entity graphs). Ship one core ML artifact and its eval per phase. Make no forecasting claims at all: GDELT measures attention, not truth, and LLM agents score only ~30 F1 on it (MIRAI).
- GPU-polish sinks before the thesis ships: a 1.5M-point bokeh target at 60 fps, float-framebuffer 4K stills, WebCodecs clips, nightly software-GL MP4 Globe Casts, Satellite Day, attention-weather particles. Use MapLibre-native layers wherever deck.gl GlobeView is experimental, and gate polish on a passing perf budget.
- Unverified dependencies on the critical path: HDX HAPI endpoints, the VIEWS licence, Media Cloud's research-oriented directory, the V2.1Counts fill rate, pre-2017 PAGE_TITLE. Spike each in week 1, start the UCDP token, ReliefWeb appname and FIRMS key registrations in week 0, and publish a sensor-only index variant so the product survives a missing source.
- Crisis-versus-crisis leaderboards or 'worth' language. Compare languages and coverage, show intervals, keep severity definitions switchable, publish an ethics statement, and get 2–3 humanitarian or journalism reviewers before launch.
- Recurring manual rituals that fail publicly when the developer pauses, such as a weekly report that needs a human every Monday. Auto-generate it behind the reproducibility gate, with sampled human review and a reviewed/unreviewed badge.
- Gold sets the developer can't credibly label, such as 1,500 items across 12 languages with 20% double annotation. Size labelling to languages with real annotators, report per-language confidence, and prefer public benchmarks such as SemEval-2022 T8 where they exist.
- Two writers in two clouds for one pipeline, like Experience's Cloud Run 'fallback writer'. Keep one writer and one ledger; the fallback is serving last-good static data, not double-writing.
- Budgets parked at the ceiling. No steady state may depend on the CPX32 fallback or on $30+/month of LLM spend; hard-cap the LLM at about $15/month using the Batch API, memoization and caching, and keep a public cost panel.
- Breadth layers (flights, ships, markets, instability or bias scores) and lines of code as a goal. The first is World Monitor's turf; the second makes a huge AI-written codebase invite 'who reviewed this?'.

## Judge: product

*Lens:* Would real people use, love and share it? (value, delight, uniqueness, coherence, shareability, accessibility/mobile)

**Verdict.** Experience wins on a tie-break. Experience and Contrarian both score 46/60; AI-native has 38, Intelligence 37 and Systems 32. Experience takes four of six criteria (craft, sharing, accessibility and daily value), and its weaknesses are execution risks that planning can fix: reorder phases, cut spectacle, add retention. The Contrarian's weakness is a measurement risk at the core of a one-question product. If sensor severity misreads chronic crises, its single claim fails in public. Experience is also the better host, because its Shot contract and bake-the-past CDN design can absorb every graft. But don't ship Experience as written. Re-spine it on the Contrarian's question, since that is what makes GlobaLens impossible to confuse with World Monitor and makes 'See beyond the headlines' literal. Viewfinder (live, surprise-encoded, optically honest attention) is the context. Exposure (press E, the empty ring, stops, per-language globes, a weekly reproducible report) is the hero and the launch story, live by Phase 3–4. Propagation arcs and decade replay with as-of honesty are the depth. Prism becomes a late, κ-gated phase, and the Director emits Globe Scripts checked by AtlasBench-style graders. From Intelligence, take account-free watch-lenses, diffs and the Sigstore ledger. From Systems, take the exactly-once ledger and chaos harness, two-clock freshness, magnitude M and how-computed popovers. From the Contrarian, take the discipline: every feature moves an exposure or validation metric. Price on the orderable CPX32, where only the Contrarian's footprint fits under $50. Keep chat, forecasting, accounts and World Monitor's commodity features out of the identity. Intelligence has the right retention brain inside a pro suite too close to World Monitor; AI-native builds on a claim World Monitor already owns; Systems is an excellent engine and a weak product.

**Must avoid:**

- Pricing the plan on Hetzner CAX21. The research confirms the Cost-Optimized CX/CAX line can't currently be ordered in Germany or Finland, and the Systems fallback (CAX11/CX33) is in the same line. On the orderable CPX32 (€35.99 with IPv4), only the Contrarian's footprint stays at or under $50/month. Budget on an orderable VM from day one and size the LLM cap to fit.
- Back-loading the differentiators. Experience puts Prism and Infrared in phases 7–8 and calls them cuttable, so one schedule slip leaves a prettier World Monitor. The attention-gap hero must be live by Phase 3–4.
- Building the product's identity or retention on things World Monitor already ships: an analyst that moves the map, WebMCP view tools, alert rules and daily digests, keyword spikes, instability scores, an embeddable live map, or flights, ships and markets.
- Marketing GDELT as live or breaking news. Filename stamps aren't publish times, and crawl lag is about 2 h by one vision's own measurement. No 'weather radar every 15 minutes' copy without the second clock.
- Spectacle that contradicts the data: orbit-to-street zoom, '1.5M points' as a goal, or Satellite Day as a default. The best geocode is a city centroid, 12–26% of articles are country-only, and about 21% have no location.
- Conflict or fatality forecasting. It contradicts 'attention isn't reality', overlaps VIEWS and conflictforecast.org, and World Monitor's forecast scores were withdrawn under audit, so it is a reputational trap.
- A chat-first identity, or any uncached LLM call on the landing path. Chat over titles and ledes loses to tools that read full articles, and per-question costs scale with success.
- Streaming AI sentences and striking them through after verification. Verify before text renders as final, or show it with pending chips; never present unsupported claims as normal prose.
- Accounts and their infrastructure before a measured need: Better Auth as a separate Hono service, Web Push, per-user 07:00 briefs, Boards. Start with URLs, Atom/webhooks and localStorage.
- Encoding the gap only as a bivariate colour map, or showing more than one colour encoding at once. Use the empty ring with −/+ glyphs, and colour-blind-check every scale.
- Ranking crises against each other while claiming not to (the Contrarian's Lens list ranked by stops contradicts its own policy). Decide the policy explicitly: per-language exposure first, intervals always, no leaderboards of suffering, reviewed by humanitarian experts.
- Treating sensor severity as complete. USGS, GDACS and FIRMS see sudden-onset hazards; chronic food insecurity and displacement need crisis-level sources (HDX HAPI endpoints to verify; INFORM Severity Index worth evaluating, licence to verify), and UCDP is coded from news. Label severity coverage per crisis, or the index fails exactly where NGOs look.
- Promising evaluation labour a solo developer can't do: 1,500 items in 12 or more languages with 20% double annotation, or 'second annotator' gold sets in general. Scope gold sets to languages you can annotate or recruit volunteers for, and publish N and κ honestly.
- Infrastructure and component padding: Dagster, pgmq, DuckLake, Better Auth, SSE and Web Push all at once; Kafka, Kubernetes or ClickHouse without a measured trigger; or counting a trigger-gated Rust crate in the LOC total or résumé before its trigger fires.
