Row anatomy

Title
Noto Serif 15/21 (16/23 on phones), in the original script. lang from the source language; dir="auto" in P1. The whole title is the link out.
Meta line
JetBrains Mono 500 caps 11/14: language · outlet · time, each in <bdi>. Time is the reader’s zone, UTC on hover.
Copies chip
“+11 copies · 4 outlets”. Counts are over the rows the Lens has loaded for this place.
Country group
One row per country or region the ring touches, with its count. Never a point on the map.
Keys
↑ ↓ move through rows, Enter opens the article, arrow keys on the globe move the ring.
Targets
Every control is at least 32 px tall.
