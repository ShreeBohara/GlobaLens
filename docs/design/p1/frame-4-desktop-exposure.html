Where the product is going, not P1 scope: Press E recolours the globe by coverage against severity. The stops readout and its sentence are the plan’s own example; crisis names are placeholders. Cells are drawn on a schematic hexagonal grid at about the spacing of H3 resolution 3, not real H3 indexes. docs/design/p3.md specifies this screen before it is built. Colours, sizes and type are the plan’s §4 tokens. The named faces (Inter, JetBrains Mono, Newsreader, Noto Serif) are not embedded: this file falls back to system fonts, so letterforms differ from the target. Land and borders: Natural Earth (public domain), projected ahead of time. No network requests, no scripts beyond the fit-to-width helper.
