Ten seconds: rest, a drag from Budapest to Kyiv with the ring fixed, release, then the settle sequence from the plan’s table. In P1 the list is newest first; the P4 and P8 beats are drawn dashed. The 150 ms and 300 ms beats are faster than the eye follows at full speed: use 0.25× or 0.1×, or drag the scrubber. Open with ?t=4150 to land on an exact instant. This sketch shows the cached case; when title shards still have to load, rows appear as they arrive and a skeleton shows only after 1 s. Colours, sizes and type are the plan’s §4 tokens. The named faces (Inter, JetBrains Mono, Newsreader, Noto Serif) are not embedded: this file falls back to system fonts, so letterforms differ from the target. Land and borders: Natural Earth (public domain), projected ahead of time. No network requests. One inline script draws the globe on a canvas.
