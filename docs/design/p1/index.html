P1 visual target

Six frames and one motion sketch for docs/design/p1.md. Three frames and the motion sketch show what Phase 1 ships. Three show where the product is going in later phases, so that P1’s tokens, type and layout are chosen with them in view. Every headline and outlet is synthetic, and every readout is illustrative.

1 · Desktop, ViewfinderP1 scope

The plan’s demo moment: Kyiv under the ring, headlines in Ukrainian, Russian, English and Polish, copies collapsed, country-level coverage as groups.

2 · The Lens listP1 scope

Scripts and direction, the copies and country-group expansions, the frozen list while dragging, the plain P1 edge states, and the tokens.

3 · Mobile drawerP1 scope

Khartoum on a phone at the three detents: 120 px, 50% and 92%. Arabic rows right to left.

4 · Desktop, ExposureTarget · P3 and P5

Press E over the Sahel: cells coloured by coverage against severity, the stops readout with its interval and sentence, empty rings.

5 · Story sheetTarget · P4

The Lens lists stories; one opens in the 420 px sheet with its headlines, glosses, languages and method.

6 · Crisis LedgerTarget · P5

A pre-rendered page for one crisis: stops by language with intervals, severity and attention with sources, the labelled AI note.

Motion · Develop on settle10 s · P1, with P4 and P8 beats marked

Drag from Budapest to Kyiv, release, and the list develops: counters at 0 ms, re-rank at about 150 ms. Scrub it or slow it to 0.1×.

How these were made. Each file is one self-contained HTML page: inline styles, an inline SVG globe, no network requests. The motion sketch draws its globe on a canvas with one inline script. Colours, sizes and type follow the plan’s §4 tokens. The named faces (Inter, JetBrains Mono, Newsreader, Noto Serif) are not embedded, so these pages fall back to system fonts and the letterforms differ from the target. Land and borders are Natural Earth (public domain). The previews on this page are reduced screenshots of the frames.
